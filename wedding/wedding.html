How it began

Our Story

Photo placeholder

We met on a rainy Tuesday in October, both reaching for the last umbrella at the corner shop. Neither of us got the umbrella — but we did get coffee, and three hours later neither of us wanted to leave.

Since then, we've built a life out of small rituals: Sunday markets, terrible karaoke, and a running list of places we still want to see together. Somewhere along the way, "someday" turned into "always."

Last spring, on the same corner where we met, Jordan finally found an umbrella worth keeping — and asked Alex to keep it too.
